### 2. What's New?

**For Admin & Salesman:**
- When creating a new booking, you'll see a "Discount" section
- Three options available:
  1. **No Discount** (default)
  2. **Percentage (%)** - Enter 0-100% discount
  3. **Fixed Amount (₹)** - Enter discount amount in rupees

**How it works:**
- Discount is calculated ONLY on Product Rent (subtotal)
- Transportation charges are NOT included in discount calculation
- Final Total = (Subtotal - Discount) + Transportation
- The discount amount is automatically calculated and stored in the database
- All discount information is saved with the booking

### 3. Example:
- Product Rent: ₹1000
- Apply 10% discount = ₹100 discount (on product rent only)
- After Discount: ₹900
- Transportation: ₹200 (added separately, no discount)
- Final Total: ₹1100

OR

- Product Rent: ₹1000
- Apply ₹150 fixed discount (on product rent only)
- After Discount: ₹850
- Transportation: ₹200 (added separately, no discount)
- Final Total: ₹1050

### 4. Database Fields Added:
- `discount_type` - 'percentage' or 'amount'
- `discount_value` - The percentage or amount entered
- `discount_amount` - Calculated discount in rupees

### 5. Discount Handling in Cancellation & Exchange:

**Full Cancellation:**
- Discount is preserved in booking record (for history)
- Refund calculated based on the final total amount customer paid (after discount)

**Partial Cancellation:**
- ⚠️ **Discount is REMOVED entirely** from the booking
- Remaining products are charged at FULL PRICE (no discount)
- Example:
  * Original: Product A (₹3,000) + Product B (₹2,000) = ₹5,000
  * With 10% discount: ₹4,500
  * Cancel Product A → Remaining: Product B at ₹2,000 (NO discount)
  * New Total: ₹2,000 + Transportation

**Product Exchange:**
- Exchange calculations use **BEFORE-DISCOUNT** product rent values
- Exchange penalty calculated on original product rent (not discounted price)
- ⚠️ **Discount is REMOVED** during exchange
- New total calculated without any discount
- Example:
  * Original Product: ₹3,000 rent (before discount)
  * Exchange Penalty: 20% × ₹3,000 = ₹600
  * New Product: ₹2,500 rent
  * New Total: ₹2,500 + Transportation (no discount applied)

**Why this approach?**
- Simple and clear for staff and customers
- Prevents complex pro-rata calculations
- Discounts are promotional - modifications forfeit the discount
- Easier to track and explain in transactions
